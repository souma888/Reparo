# Data and ML scope

AI is a direct Gemini multimodal evidence organizer, not a trained Reparo classifier. Recognition is qualitative, hypotheses are unverified, and no calibrated probabilities are shown. Provider requests carry only user-consented evidence. No training dataset is collected by this app. Google's free-tier data terms are explained at submission.

Local manual retrieval extracts text from user-selected PDF/text files, filters by exact normalized model, ranks keyword overlap, and returns up to three passage/page references. Only supplied model-matched references can appear as AI citations. User-entered manufacturer/model/source metadata is unverified. Files remain local; excerpts are sent to AI only with the additional opt-in.

The hazard corpus and synthetic retrieval fixtures are regression evals. They do not establish real-world sensitivity, diagnosis accuracy or manufacturer applicability. An expert-labeled manual/device benchmark and held-out real-device evaluation remain follow-up work.
